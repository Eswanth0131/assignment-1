Maximum Likelihood (ML) Estimation of conditional models










The discussion in the marginal distribution is equivalently applicable to the conditional distribution 
\(p_{model}(\mathbf y | \mathbf x, \mathbf w)\)
 which governs supervised learning, 
\(y\)
 being the symbol of the label / target variable. Therefore all machine learning software frameworks offer excellent APIs on CE calculation.


\[ L(\mathbf w) = - \mathbb{E_{\mathbf x, \mathbf y \sim \hat p_{data}}}  \log p_{model}(\mathbf y | \mathbf x; \mathbf w)\]


The attractiveness of the ML solution is that the CE (also known as log-loss) is general and we 
don’t need to re-design it
 when we change the model.
